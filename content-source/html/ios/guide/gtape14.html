Conclusion

There is no final conclusion to recovery, but I would like to conclude this app with a bit of experience sharing.

Men for men, women for women - I was a bit baffled when old timers were stressing this principle to me in my early days. But as I kept coming back, I came to a realization that this was good for my humility. I learnt that the fellowship is a place for sick people to come together and help each other. Some stay sick even after putting the drink down and are able to make others more sicker. Today, I do have friends of the opposite sex in the fellowship, but I like to keep them at a healthy distance. I try not to travel with them to meetings if they are alone and I also try to protect the new comes from the predators in the fellowship. Afterall, AA has taught me that I am responsible.
