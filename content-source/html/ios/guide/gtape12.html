Step 11

Sought through prayer and meditation to improve our conscious contact with God as we understood Him, praying only for knowledge of His will for us and the power to carry that out.

My experience with this step is that I can use it first thing in the morning, all throughout the day and before I go to bed in the night. Prayer means I talk to my higher power and meditation means listening, perhaps to my higher power.

In my early days, I was told that just sitting at a meeting was meditation in itself as I'm listening to other people. The word meditate in itself means to focus. If I can sit at a meeting a listen, that's good meditation in itself to start with. Gradually I came to believe and my higher power made conscious contact with me after which I started listening to my higher power.

This power was guiding me way before I had picked up my first drink ever. But it was only when I worked the steps and lost enough self will that I truly and consciously was aware of this power.

Our book also says that everyone in the fellowship has their own individual journeys with this step. Some people join religious institutions, some join spiritual instutions. But I remember my sponsor saying to me that as long as I kept AA above all, I would not pick up a drink and my life would keep getting better.

The second part of this step suggests that I only ask God what his will is for me. In the simplest form, I came to understand that this means doing the next right thing. Whenever I'm confused, I simply keep doing the next right thing. Sometimes this can be doing the dishes, doing the laundry, going for a walk or going to a meeting. But there is always something I can do instead of listening to my crazy thoughts.

I've finally stopped praying to God for a bigger house, a bigger car, a better salary etc. I now ask God to direct my thinking, to keep my mind open and to keep making myself better one day at a time. Over a period of time by the practice of these principles, I quit smoking. It has been a good few days now that I haven't even bothered to think about that habit.

One day in my meditation, a thought came to me that perhaps I could start living a better life, physically. I've been going to the gym and doing a bit of running ever since. I managed to shed a lot of weight and feel lot better physically. As this illness is three fold - mental, physical and spiritual, if I'm in a fit physically condition also, there's a better chance that I can beat this illness just for the day.

Finally, before I go to bed I always like to take an inventory of my day and look at my behaviours. Where have I been resentful, selfish, dishonest or afraid. Whether I've hurt someone in my day. Whether my day was productive and what I could have done to make it better. Whatever I do, I do not immerse myself into self pity. If there's anything that is bothering me, I can always pray over it and talk to my sponsor the following morning if it can wait.

Throughout the day, as long as I keep remembering the fact that i'm not running my life anymore - my higher power is, I stand a chance to have a reasonably peaceful and happy day.

Faith without works is dead!
