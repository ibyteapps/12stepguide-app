# Step 12 Carrying The Message

As the step literally suggests, all we do now is keep carrying this message to other alcoholics. This means, going to regular meetings, staying in service, working with a sponsor and sponsees.

As developers, we will be adding a whole hoard of features to this section in the coming months. Keep an eye out for our next major release.

List of future enhancements include a comprehensive 12th step where you will be able to journal every meeting you have been to and every 12 step call you have attended.

We are also including a sleek meeting finder into the app which will allow you to search our database of about 100,000 AA meetings.

Keep Coming Back. It Works If You Work It!
